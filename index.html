Europe as Our Core

Why Europe

Europe has a choice. The continent that pioneered the industrial revolution now risks becoming its footnote. Not from lack of talent or expertise but from failure of will and ambition.

We invest in European prosperity because computing is the bedrock of economic growth. Every major industry runs on semiconductors. Every breakthrough in medicine, energy, transport and communications depends on advances in processing power.

This isn't a vertical to put a little bit of money towards. Semiconductors are everything. Europe invented much of this technology within living memory. It can lead again if we choose to do so.

"We want to see the next trillion dollar company in Europe. We think it will be a computing hardware company. We are here to back the founders who are going to build it."

Global R&D Talent

Europe has world-class research institutions and engineering talent in semiconductors, photonics and advanced materials.

Industrial Heritage

Home to ASML, NXP, Infineon, STMicroelectronics, and hundreds of deep tech research centres and fabs.

The Missing Piece

Europe has everything it needs to win globally in semiconductors. What it has been missing is dedicated, specialist early-stage capital.

Policy Tailwinds

The EU Chips Act and national semiconductor strategies are creating unprecedented momentum for European chip companies.

Focus Areas

What We Invest In

Three interconnected domains at the heart of semiconductor innovation. We invest across the full value chain: from the design software and simulation tools that architect new chips, to the equipment and measurement systems that make production possible, all the way to the devices, materials and integration platforms that power the end product.

01

Semiconductors

Compute & Electronics

We invest in compute and control platforms that deliver more performance per watt and reshape where and how computation happens, from space, to datacentres, wearables, and medical devices.

02

Photonics

Connectivity & Sensing

We back photonic and mixed electro-optic systems that move, measure and process information: from high-bandwidth communications to imaging, diagnostics and instrumentation.

03

Advanced Materials

Equipment, Design Tools & Metrology

We invest in advanced materials, equipment, design tools and metrology that extend what fabs and manufacturers can do, turning lab-scale innovations into robust, repeatable production.
